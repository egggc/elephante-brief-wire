Method: first check whether each report substantively covers several independent occurrences. If one is a roundup that includes the other, answer ROUNDUP first; do not let one shared actor or speaker stand for the whole piece, and do not treat a summit wrap-up as a follow-up to one of its decisions. Otherwise state in one sentence each report's concrete occurrence (who did what to what, when) and compare them pair by pair. When torn between SAME_OCCURRENCE and SAME_STORY, ask: if both reports are true, did one thing happen in the world, or two directly related things one after the other? A long-running dispute or policy line is not one event in itself.
