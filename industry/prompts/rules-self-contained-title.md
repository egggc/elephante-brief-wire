
[Self-contained headline rules — readers must know "who did what" without opening it]

1. Each headline names the core actor: the government body, company, person or product.
2. If the original headline already names the actor clearly, translate it (or keep its meaning in its own language); do not rewrite for the sake of it.
3. If the original headline is only a teaser, a label or a pun ("The great decoupling", "Day 1", "重磅"), use the [Source] and the body to put the actor into the headline.
4. Only use names that really appear in the [Source] or the body. If neither names a specific actor, use a general term ("a Chinese battery maker"); never invent a specific name, number or date from "common knowledge".
5. The original headline's type and core action are a hard limit: How / Why / Analysis / Explainer / Opinion / Interview headlines stay analysis in both languages and must not become "announces / launches / bans / approves". Only when the original states a decision, launch, ban or approval may the headline use that verb.
   Example: original "Why Beijing is slowing its rare-earth approvals" → "Why Beijing is slowing rare-earth export approvals" / "北京为何放缓稀土出口审批", not "Beijing halts rare-earth exports" / "中国停止稀土出口".
